The [blogroll](/) on the homepage is a great way to see who's here, but today
I launched a second way to read what everyone is writing. A river!

The difference: the blogroll is a list of *feeds* you open one at a time. A
river is the posts themselves — every member's newest items in one stream,
newest first, with the text right there. Both are built from the same
[members' OPML list](/subscriptions.opml).

Head over to [River](/river) in the header and scan the latest.

It runs Dave Winer's river software from FeedLand, same as the blogroll. Thanks Dave!
